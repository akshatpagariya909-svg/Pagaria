We're a few architects, and finding architecture online kept frustrating us. So we built Discover Architecture: one big wall of 1,600 buildings you wander instead of search.

Shuffle it, hit Surprise me, open any building for photos, key features and links to drawings and articles, or filter by idea, type, movement, region and era. Every photo is free and credited.

It's early and we're adding more all the time. Link in bio. Tell us what we should add next.
