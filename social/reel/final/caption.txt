Discovering architecture online got dumbed down. So we built this. 🧱

We're a few architects who kept hitting the same wall: Pinterest is beautiful but nameless, and most sites need you to know a building before you can find it.

Discover Architecture is one big wall of 1,600 buildings you wander instead of search.
→ Shuffle it, or hit Surprise me
→ Open any building for photos, key features and links to drawings and articles
→ Filter by idea, type, movement, region and era

Free. No sign-up. Every photo credited. Link in bio.
