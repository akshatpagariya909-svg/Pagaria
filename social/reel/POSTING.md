# Posting the launch reel

Files: the reel (`discover-architecture-reel-final.mp4`, sent in chat), `cover.jpg`, `caption.txt`.

## Before posting (once)
- Professional account (Creator, category Education), bio and link done from the setup checklist.
- Bio link: `https://akshatpagariya909-svg.github.io/Pagaria/?ref=instagram` (the `?ref=` tag lets the visit counter show Instagram visits once it is added).

## Posting
1. New post → Reel → choose the video. Don't add filters or trim it.
2. Cover: "Add from camera roll" → `cover.jpg`. Check the profile-grid preview shows the wordmark and the line.
3. Caption: paste `caption.txt` as is (it is under Instagram's 2,200-character limit and includes the photo credits).
4. Rename the audio from "Original audio" to "Discover Architecture" (tap the audio name before sharing). Others can reuse it, which credits you.
5. Advanced settings → turn on **AI label** (the voice is AI-generated). Leave "Share to Facebook" as you like.
6. Location: leave empty, or your city.
7. Share. Then: ⋯ → Pin to your profile.

## First hour (this matters most)
- Reply to every comment, with a question back where you can.
- Share the reel to your story with a "Link" sticker to the site (stories allow clickable links).
- Send it directly to 10–20 people who love buildings (send, don't ask them to like): DMs and shares count for reach more than likes.
- Post when your friends and their friends are online, e.g. a weekday evening in your time zone.

## The next days
- Post the first carousels every one to two days after the reel (order in the setup checklist).
- Check Insights after 24 hours and after 7 days: plays, average watch time, shares, saves, profile activity, link taps.
- If the first 3 seconds lose most viewers (Insights → retention), try the other opening line next time.
