Unscroll

A friend who notices when you've been scrolling a while — and keeps noticing.

Problem Screen Time nudges once. You tap "Ignore limit for 15 minutes" and you're on your own again.
Our bet Constant, quiet, good-looking visual nudges that get a little more honest the longer you stay. No sound, no video, no guilt trips.
Measure Make the scroll countable: visits, pickups, minutes per session, and a thumb odometer that turns time into distance.
Nudge — constantly A Dynamic Island companion that ticks along while you scroll, plus a shield whose copy changes every time you come back.
Reflect Widgets and a weekly receipt that make the habit visible — and count the times you walked away, not just the times you didn't.
iOS reality check
What iOS lets us do Screen Time API (usage thresholds, custom shields, pickups) · Shortcuts "app opened" automations for visit counts · Live Activities & Dynamic Island · WidgetKit · Focus filters
What it won't allow Floating windows on top of other apps · reading real scrolls, taps or reel counts inside Instagram/YouTube. So distance is an estimate on iOS; real counts are an Android-only feature.
