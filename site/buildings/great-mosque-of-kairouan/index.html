Great Mosque of Kairouan

Great Mosque of Kairouan is a religious building in Kairouan, Tunisia, by Uqba ibn Nafi; Aghlabid builders, dating from 836. Shown here in a photograph by MAREK SZAREJKO from CLONMEL, IRELAND - POLAND.
