Salisbury Cathedral

Salisbury Cathedral is a religious building in Salisbury, United Kingdom, by Elias of Dereham (attrib.), dating from 1258. Shown here through Salisbury Cathedral from the Bishop’s Grounds, a painting by John Constable, 1823.
