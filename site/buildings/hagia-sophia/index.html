Hagia Sophia

Hagia Sophia is a religious building in Istanbul, Türkiye, by Isidore of Miletus, Anthemius of Tralles, dating from 537. Shown here through Aya Sofia, Constantinople, a print by Louis Haghe after Gaspare Fossati, 1852.
