About this wall

Find Great Architecture is a huge, growing wall of buildings for architecture students, from the modern masters to contemporary and parametric work, brutalism and regionalism, with India and South Asia first. Every building has free photos with credits, its key features, and links to plans, sections and articles for a case study. Buildings from all over the world; see them all A–Z.
