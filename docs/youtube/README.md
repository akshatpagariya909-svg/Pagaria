# YouTube channel kit: Find Great Architecture

Made 15 Oct 2026. Nothing here is live yet: the channel has to be created from the owner's Google account.

Files in this folder:
- `profile-800.png`: profile picture (FGA, with the red dot). YouTube shows it as a circle.
- `banner-2560x1440.png`: channel banner. The name and line sit inside the area every device shows (centre 1546 × 423).

## Create the channel (about 5 minutes, on a phone or computer)

1. Sign in to YouTube with the Google account you want to own it (a separate one for the site is tidier than a personal one).
2. Click your picture → **Create a channel** → name it **Find Great Architecture**.
3. **Customisation → Profile**: upload `profile-800.png`, paste the handle and description below, add the links.
4. **Customisation → Branding**: upload `banner-2560x1440.png`.
5. Settings → **Channel → Feature eligibility**: verify your phone number (needed for custom thumbnails and videos over 15 minutes).
6. Tell Claude the channel address and it will add the link to the site (small, footer or About, checked at 400px and desktop first).

**Channel keywords:** architecture, architecture students, case study, brutalism, parametric architecture, building tour, architecture quiz

**Country:** your own. **Business email:** add one you check, so people can reach you.

## Video description template

> [Building], [Architect], [City], [Year]
> Key features: [one line from the site]
> See it on the wall, with plans and sections: https://findgreatarchitecture.com/buildings/[id]/
>
> Photos: [Photographer], [Licence], [Commons link]. (one line per photo)

Every photo must be free (public domain, CC0, CC BY, CC BY-SA from Wikimedia Commons). CC BY and CC BY-SA require the credit and licence in the description, same rule as the site. No NC or ND, no licensed photos, no music you don't own (use YouTube's Audio Library or no music).

## First five videos (Shorts, 15–45 seconds, vertical)

1. **Daily five, played live**: screen-record one round of the game; end on the share card and the link.
2. **One building, three things to notice**: the Key features line over 3 free photos.
3. **Guess the building**: show a photo cropped, reveal after 5 seconds.
4. **Scroll the wall**: a calm screen recording of the wall on a phone, no talking, ends on the name and URL.
5. **Same architect, three buildings**: from a "More by [architect]" panel.

Start with Shorts: they need no editing skill, they suit a phone-first audience, and they send people to the site.
