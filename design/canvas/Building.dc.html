CASA GILARDI

LUIS BARRAGÁN · MEXICO CITY, MEXICO · 1976

A house where colour, water and architecture become one experience.

Barragán's last house is built around a jacaranda tree on a narrow city lot. A corridor washed in yellow light leads to the dining room, where the floor gives way to a pool and a single red wall stands in the water.
